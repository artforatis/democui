FAQ

Good to know.

What is a Soul Pass?

A soulbound token on HyperEVM. It stays in the wallet that minted it and cannot be sold or transferred.

Can I mint more than one pass?

Yes, in order. Silver unlocks Gold, and Gold unlocks Diamond. Your highest pass decides your access on Elysium.

How do invites work?

You get an invite link after you join, and it is added to your posts automatically. Gold needs 3 friends and Diamond needs 7.

What does minting cost?

$0.66 worth of $KNTQ per pass, plus a small HYPE gas fee on HyperEVM.

When does Elysium launch?

Kinetiq has announced Elysium mainnet for November 9, 2026. The snapshot happens before that.
